This panel is where you can export your design as usable files. You can export various components of your instrument as PDF, SVG, DXF, and in some cases STL files. 

## Bundles
**Save Recipe Data** saves the design itself, to reopen later or share. **Full Plan PDF** prints every sheet and template on one paper size, chosen to fit the largest piece. **Templates SVG** includes the pieces commonly cut out on thin materials. **Full SVG** is every panel render in one file.

## Sections
Each row below can be previewed on the canvas or saved as SVG, PDF or DXF. Any row the design hasn't reached yet is greyed out until its panel has been completed. 

## 3D
The top and back plates export as STL, which can be uploaded to various CNC programs.
