The circle that gives a bout its sides. The two of them, mirrored across the centreline, overlap in the lens a draughtsman calls a vesica piscis.

In each of the [[bouts]] the vesica circle is placed tangent to the bout's widest line and tangent inside the join arc, so its radius alone sets how full the bout's shoulder is: a large radius flattens the side, a small one rounds it. The field labelled *Vesecai Radius* is this radius.
