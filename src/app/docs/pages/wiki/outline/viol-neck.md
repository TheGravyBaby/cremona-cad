A flat top to the upper bout, as on a double bass or a viola da gamba, in place of the join arc.

With it on, the body ends in a flat face of the given width, and the arc V0 turns the corner down into the bout's [[vesica]] arc. The join radius rounds the corner between the flat and V0; zero leaves it sharp. Still in development.
