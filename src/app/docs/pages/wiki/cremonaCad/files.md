A design is stored in a single JSON file holding every parameter and all reference images, so it reopens exactly as you saved it.

## Saving and opening
Use the file menu to load and save recipes in progress.

Your work in progress lives in the browser tab. Each tab is its own workspace, and closing it discards the work. Saving to your computer is the only durable copy.

## Templates
Choose a template designed from the historical instruments at the Library of Congress or start from a blank slate. A blank instrument will grow as you make it. As you move through the panels, defualt values are calculated using common ratios against your previous inputs. 

## Exports
Templates, plans and 3D surfaces are produced on the [[export]] panel. Layers and selections can be saved from the bottom bar as SVG or DXF, see [[layers]].

## Undo and redo
*Ctrl + Z* undoes and *Ctrl + Shift + Z* or *Ctrl + Y* redoes, up to fifty steps. Edits to the panels and shapes share one timeline, so undo reaches whichever you did last.
