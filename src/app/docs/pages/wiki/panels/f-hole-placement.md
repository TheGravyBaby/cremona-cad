This panel allows you to set the placement of the f-hole eyes and its central stem. F-hole geometry can become quite complex. The community is full of theories regarding how to position the eyes relative to the body, there seems to be no commonly agreed on method. 

F-holes all feature and upper and lower eye, along with a straight stem section. CremonaCad tries to keep things flexible just setting these positions as XY coordinate values. 

## Notches
If you want your f-holes to feature notches, you may toggle this feature on. The body stop distance for the inner notch matches the Y value of your stem. The outer notch is placed using a guiding arc centered on the lower wing. To create a parametric model that could accuraely represent a wide variety of notches would have involved adding more parameters to an already very dense set. Notches are traditionally cut with a knife after an f-hole has been cut, a fairly straightforward task in the violin making process. Ergo, this is a nice to have feature, but if you need more, feel free to program your own CAD tool.

## View Toggles
Given the number of f-hole placement theories available, this panel comes with a few view toggles which may help you place your eyes. The "Show Placement Guides" toggle show you the distance your lower eye travels from the corner height, as well as the lower eyes distance from the rib line. The "Show Beard Guides" toggle renders guide boxes for the center 1/3, 2/9 and 1/9 of the instrument. These guide boxes align well with some instruments.
