The panel section is on the right side of the screen, and it holds all of the instrument properties. The panels are ordered in accordance with the process one would use to make a real instrument. Each panel holds the parameters for its section and draws them on the canvas. By modifying the properies on a panel, you will see the canvas update in real time. 

## Moving through the panels
To move through the recipe steps, use the Back and Next buttons. The first time a panel opens it seeds its values in proportion to the stages before it, so there is always something on the canvas to edit.

## Locked panels
A panel stays locked until the stage it builds on has been solved. The corners need the bouts, the center bout needs the corners, the arching needs the fluting channel, and so on. If a panel you expect is greyed out, go back and finish the ones before it.

## View toggles
On the very bottom of the panel  there are several view toggles. These toggles can provide relevant reference infomation to help you place your design. 

## Failures and warnings
When a value can't be drawn, the panel will throw an error which will appear on the top bar. In many cases, the errored geometry will be rendered on the canvas to help you identify the problem.
