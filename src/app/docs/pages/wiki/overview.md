Welcome to CremonaCad, a one-of-a-kind tool for designing violins! This article will provide a high-level overview of the UI and a guide on how to get started. 


## Getting your bearings
Most of CremonaCad's UI elements are located on the edges of the screen. These elements draw and modify shapes which are rendered on the canvas. 

|--|--|
| Canvas | Located in the center of the screen, the [[canvas]] is a flat XY plane where all objects are rendered. To learn about navigating the canvas, please see the [[controls]] article. |
| Recipe Panels | Located on the right of the screen, the recipe panels contain the various parameters and options used in designing your instrument. |
| Toolbox | Located on the left side of the screen, the toolbox contains a large library of drafting [[tools]] that can assist you in designing your instrument. |
| Top Bar | The top bar contains high-level controls for file selection, settings, the notification system, as well as access to these docs. |
| Bottom Bar | The bottom bar contains high-level settings for the canvas, such as controls for [[layers]] and reference [[images]]. It also displays settings for selected canvas objects. |

## Starting your instrument
### Choosing a reference
CremonaCad comes with several historical templates, but of course the fun in this is designing your own. A template can be a starting point: open one and change whatever you like. Still, most makers at least start with a proven historical reference before beginning an instrument. To start, it is recommended that you find not only images, but also measurements for your reference instrument. Many online sources, such as Tarisio, will often publish measurements of the body along with their images. 

### Adding the image
Paste or upload your reference image onto the canvas, then scale it against the bounding box on the base measurement panel. See [[images]] for the details.

### Drafting
With the scaled reference image, you can begin creating your new instrument. On the recipe panel, select the next button to go through the various recipe steps. You can find more details about each panel in the recipe [[panel]] section. CremonaCad is designed around keyboard use. It is recommended to click into a value and use the arrow keys to step. If you click into an XY coordinate value, up and down arrows move you up and down while left and right function the same way.
