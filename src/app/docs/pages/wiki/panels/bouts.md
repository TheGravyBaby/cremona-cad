The upper and lower bouts: the two wide sections of the body, and the first arcs of the outline.

![The three widths, each measured outside the ribs.](wiki/bout-widths.svg)

Each bout's width is an outer measurement, including rib thickness and overhang. The arcs themselves are drawn inset from it by that amount, since the outline being drafted is the rib line the mould is cut to.

A bout is three arcs. The join arc sits on the centreline, tangent to the end of the body. A [[vesica]] arc runs down each side, tangent to the bout's widest line and tucked inside the join arc. The outline passes from one to the other where they touch.

On a double bass or a gamba the upper bout's join arc gives way to a [[viol-neck]].
