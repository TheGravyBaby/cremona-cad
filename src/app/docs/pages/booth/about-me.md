Hello there! I am a musician and software engineer based out of New Haven, CT.

**CremonaCad** started as a personal tool to help me better understand the geometry behind historical violins. After months of development and experimentation, I realized the tool might have some value to the making community at large.


CremonaCad is open source, and I welcome feedback, ideas, and collaboration from anyone in the making community.
