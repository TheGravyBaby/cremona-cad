A small quality of life update. 

- **Viol neck purfling** — Improvements made to the viol neck its purfling paths. Viol neck purflings now take an arc parameter to make purfling more consistent with actual double basses. 

- **Exports** — Paths drawn from mathematical curves (catenaries, cycloids, etc) have had the number of points used to define them siginficantly reduced. This should clean up exports to other programs. 

- **Performance** - Several performance improvements related to bi-arcs, path interpolation, and caching systems.
