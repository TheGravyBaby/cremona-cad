The recipe panels on the right of the screen are the drafting stages. The panels are ordered in accordance with the process one would use to make a real instrument. Each panel holds the parameters for its stage and draws them on the canvas.

## Moving through the panels
Use the back and next buttons at the top of the panel, or select a panel from the dropdown. The first time a panel opens it seeds its values in proportion to the stages before it, so there is always something on the canvas to edit.

## Locked panels
A panel stays locked until the stage it builds on has been solved. The corners need the bouts, the center bout needs the corners, the arching needs the fluting channel, and so on. If a panel you expect is greyed out, go back and finish the ones before it.

## View toggles
At the very bottom of the panel controls exist various view toggles. These toggles can provide relevant reference infomation to help you place your design. 

## Failures and warnings
When a value can't be drawn, the panel will throw an error which will appear on the top bar. In many cases, the errored geometry will be rendered on the canvas to help you identify the error.
