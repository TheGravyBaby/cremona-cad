Hello there! I am a musician and software engineer based out of New Haven, CT.

**CremonaCad** started as a personal tool to help me understand the geometry behind historical violin patterns. After months of development and experimentation, I realized that it might be genuinely useful in developing the craft of violin-making.

I plan to keep developing the application with features like arching templates, f-hole patterns, and neck profiles. As of Spring 2026, Cremona Cad is still early in its development lifecycle, I hope to use it as an actual tool in the making of my first instruments.

CremonaCad is open source, and I welcome feedback, ideas, and collaboration from anyone in the making community.
