The canvas is a two dimensional plane with a millimeter coordinate system. Everything is rendered to scale. 

## Moving around
Scroll to zoom, drag with the middle button or *Space* to pan, and press *F* to fit everything in view. The full list is in [[controls]]. The zoom buttons at the bottom right also let you type a center and zoom to go straight to a spot.

## Axes and grid
The axes and grid button on the bottom bar toggles the axes lines and turns the X and Y grids on and off. You may change the granularity of the gridlines by modifying the step value in millimeters.  

## Snapping
When using a drawing tool, shapes will snap to exisiting geometry. 

## Selecting and editing
Click a shape to select it, drag an area to select several, and *Shift + click* to add or remove a shape to the exisiting selection. The settings bar at the bottom of the screen shows the selection's properties. Right-click the canvas to access additional selection items. 

## What's shown where
What appears on the canvas depends on the open panel: each panel draws its own geometry onto the canvas. Reference [[images]] and [[layers]] can be rendered on individual panels.
