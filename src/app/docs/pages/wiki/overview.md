Welcome to CremonaCad, a one of a kind tool for designing violins! This article will provide a high-level overview of the UI and a guide on how to get started. 


## Getting your bearings
Most of CremonaCad's UI elements are located on the edges of the screen. These elements draw and modify shapes which are rendered on the canvas. 

|--|--|
| Canvas | Located in the center of the screen, the [[canvas]] is a flat XY plane where all objects are rendered. To learn about navigating the canvas, please see the [[controls]] article. |
| Recipe Panels | Located on the right of the screen, the recipe panels contain the various parameters and options used in designing your instrument. |
| Toolbox | Located on the left side of the screen, the toolbox contains a large library of drafting [[tools]] that can assist you in designing your instrument. |
| Top Bar | The top bar contains high-level controls for file selection, settings, the notification system, as well as access to these docs. |
| Bottom Bar | The bottom bar contains high-level settings for the canvas, such as controls for [[layers]] and reference [[images]]. It also displays settings for selected canvas objects. |

## Starting your instrument
### Choosing a reference
CremonaCad comes with several historical templates, but of course the fun in this is designing your own. Still, most makers at least start with a proven historical reference before beginning an instrument. To start, it is recommended that you find not only images, but also measurements for your reference instrument. Many online sources, such as Tarisio, will often publish measurements of the body along with their images. 

### Adding the image
Once you have your reference image, you can paste it onto the canvas, or upload it from your machine. To do this, select the reference image tool on the bottom bar, and then click upload. 

### Scaling the image
The **base measurements** panel will allow you to enter the total height and width of your instrument, and will then render a bounding box of this size on the canvas. Use this bounding box to scale your reference image. Select the image and use the handles on the corners and sides to scale it. 

You may also scale a reference image by drawing measurement lines over known distances, which may help with some images. To do this, go into the toolbox and select the distance tool. Then draw a line across a known distance, which will be reported above the line. You may also edit the distance manually, select the line and edit the distance text box on the bottom bar. Once you have the known length, the reference image can be scaled using its handles.

Do keep in mind [[perspective]] when scaling images. 

### Drafting
With the scaled reference image, you can begin creating your new instrument. On the recipe panel, select the next button to go through the various recipe steps. You can find more details about each panel in the recipe [[panel]] section.
