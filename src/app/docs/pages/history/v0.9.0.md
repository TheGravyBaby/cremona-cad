- **F-hole notches** — the nicks in the stem's edges are drawn on both f-hole panels, the inner centred where the stop line crosses the inner edge and the outer where an arc struck from the lower wing's tip through the inner notch meets the outer edge, sized from the stem's width. A Notches box on the placement panel's stem turns them off, and the file remembers it; the top plate and the f-hole template carry them while it is on. The placement panel reads off the stop at the notch line; the bridge itself still stands where String Setup puts it.
- **One stem radius** — the f-hole's four stem arcs always share the one Arc Radius; the Unique radii switch and its four fields are gone from the contours panel and the file.
- **Arcs by sweep** — an arc is placed by how far it turns, in degrees, rather than by the bearing it ends on: a compound corner's second arc, an f-hole arm's split, the scroll's crown, back and front arcs, and a viol neck's join and flank. The gallery's instruments carry the sweeps. An f-hole's shoulders still end on a bearing (U1 End, L1 End, seeded at 90° and 270°), so a new radius hands over to the arm in the same place.
- **A File button** — the file menu opens from a labelled File button at the left of the name, rather than a chevron that sat just above the panel picker's. Ctrl+S (⌘S) saves and Ctrl+O (⌘O) loads. Saving always asks for a name, and the name chosen becomes the design's.
- **A new logo** — the C-hole is redrawn from its own construction, its eyes on one axis and its nick on the other, in front of the name in Roman capitals. The browser tab carries the C-hole too.
- **One recipe** — the Enrico Ceruti recipe is retired in favour of Violin Family, the same model rebuilt so every stage is solved afresh from what you type. A file saved from it opens as a Violin Family instrument, drawn the same; save it again to keep it in the new form.
- **One palette for the drawing** — the colours on canvas come from five inks and a tone per part rather than a hand-picked hex each, so a part another panel owns draws in plain grey and the day canvas gets the same ramps in a legible band. Some colours have shifted slightly.
- **Arch templates work from either face** — each long and cross template is now a strip with the arch on both edges, so one edge fits over the outside of the plate and the other into an arch carved from the inside. Templates run from takeoff to takeoff, where the arch meets the fluting.
- **Bundled SVG exports** — Templates SVG packs the neck template, the scroll's three views, scroll back, compass walk, arch templates and blocks onto one sheet, each set kept together as it previews; Full SVG packs every sheet.
- **Full plan PDF on one paper size** — every page prints on the same paper, and the templates share pages where they fit, so the small pages are gone.
- **Scroll side profile export** — the head from the side, as SVG, PDF or DXF, and in the full plan.
- **Settings menu** — the gear in the top bar holds appearance, auto-hide and a new option to fit the view each time another panel opens. Settings now carry across tabs. The **?** button opens the Info Booth: the app, the wiki, its history, acknowledgements and the author.
