Reference images sit on the canvas under your drawing. Each can be shown on some panels and hidden on others, so a photo of the scroll stays on the scroll panels, but doesn't get in the way of your bout image. 

## Adding an image
Paste an image onto the canvas, or open the image menu on the bottom bar and upload one from your computer. You may also provide a URL. Note that if your image uses a URL, you will not be able to view it offline. 

## Scaling an image
The base measurements panel draws a bounding box at the height and width you enter. Select the image and drag its handles until the instrument fills the box. You can also type a real width or height into the settings bar while the image is selected.

Where the box isn't enough, draw a distance line over a known measurement with the distance tool, then scale the image until the line reads correctly.

Do keep perspective in mind when scaling a photograph: only a shot taken square to the instrument scales evenly. Even then there will always be some distortion in images. 

## Panel viewing
By default, images are displayed no matter which recipe panel you are on. However, sometimes you might only want to display an image on a single panel, or a set of panels. To do this, go the the image menu and select the panel drop down. Here you can select which panels you would like the layers to display.
