This panel is where you can export your design as usable files. You can export various components of your instrument as PDF, SVG, DXF, and in some cases STL files. 

## Bundles
**Save Recipe Data** saves the design itself, to reopen later or share. **Schematic PDF** is a few A4 pages to print at home and read at the bench: the body, the arching, the f-holes, the neck and the head, each reduced to fit and carrying its measurements. **Scaled Drawings PDF** prints every sheet and template at full size on one paper size, chosen to fit the largest piece, with a millimetre scale down the left edge and along the bottom. **Preview** beside either lays its pages out on the canvas. **Templates SVG** includes the pieces commonly cut out on thin materials. **Full SVG** is every panel render in one file.

## Sections
Each row below can be previewed on the canvas or saved as SVG, PDF or DXF. Any row the design hasn't reached yet is greyed out until its panel has been completed. 

## 3D
The top and back plates export as STL, which can be uploaded to various CNC programs.
