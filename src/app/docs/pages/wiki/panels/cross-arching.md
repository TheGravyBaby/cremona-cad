This panel allows you to define the cross arching on the top and back plates. Traditionally, the long arch defines the peak of the arch in the middle of the instrument. At every station, cross arches rise from the edge to meet it. Cross arches therefore are bound by the height of the long arch, and the width of the instrument at their particular "station."

## Curated Cycloids
![A curtate cycloid: the path of a point inside a circle rolling along a line.](wiki/cycloid.svg)
Along with catenary curves and splines, which are detailed more in the [long arching] panel, the cross arching panel allows you to draw curated cycloids. This is another lively topic within the making community, and there doesn't seem to be any direct evidence Cremonese makers used this shape. That said, it can track some instruments cross arching quite well, and thus has some relevancy in making.
