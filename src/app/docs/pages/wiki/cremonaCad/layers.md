The layer list opens from the bottom bar. All objects drawn on the canvas must exist on a layer, including the objects drawn by the recipe panel. You may lock and unlock layers, as well as chose which layers to display using the eye icon. 

## The recipe layer
Everything the recipe panels draw sits on its own recipe layer. This layer is locked by default, but you can unlock it and then select the various shapes. You may not edit the layer, as that is the domain of the panel, but you may copy its shapes and make your own edits. 

## Panel viewing
By default, layers are displayed no matter which recipe panel you are on. However, sometimes you might only want to display a layer on a single panel, or a set of panels. To do this, go the the layer menu and select the panel drop down. Here you can select which panels you would like the layers to display.

## Import and export
You may also import / export layers as SVGs and DXFs. You will find these controls in the layer menu.
